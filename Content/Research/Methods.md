> # The Role of Faith
> 
> I see two extremes: skeptical and dogmatic. Having tread close to both ends, I find that the confluence of the two to be the most condusive for one intent on the truth. For truth cannot be ascertained by one who does not know, and for that man he depends on the words of others. Depending on this to a fault would make him fully dependent on the reality of those words, and rejecting them entirely would leave him in ignorance. So I travel along the middle, armed with a skeptical mind yet driven forward by faith, ready to accept whatever I may come to know along the way, free of bias.
> 
> This is how I might claim to remain well unbiased despite my qualified faith the Buddha. I will always remain dedicated to the truth, however that truth may contradict my faith.
