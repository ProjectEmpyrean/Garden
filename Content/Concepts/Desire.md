> ## The Precondition of Desire
> Among all experiences of stress is a certain precondition upon which the experience is brought about: desire. This desire manifests itself in shades of attraction and aversion to experience. In the case of boredom, there is an aversion to the current experience, and an attraction for some form of  entertainment. In the case of hunger, an aversion to the feeling of hunger and the concept of malnourishment, and an attraction to food which would satiate that hunger, ther scents and flavors. Without desire, even the feeling of pain would not be experienced as stressful, for there is no internal objection to it.

> # Theory of Desire
> 
> What is the source of desire? When there is an apparent desire, consciousness of its corresponding object brings about its gratification. When the desire is not apparent, still, consciousness of the same object can still bring about gratification, as if the desire was latent and only made active upon contact with its object.
> 
> As desire is being continually gratified, there is a great resolution and attenuation of desires that would otherwise be present. How does this all work? By understanding the machanics of desire, an ideal being and world can be constructed such that there is a continual gratification of a high degree.
