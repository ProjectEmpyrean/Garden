> ## The Psychology of Discomfort
> - All bad experiences are uncomfortable
> - So the end of discomfort is the end of everything affectively bad
> - Everything uncomfortable is felt as unpleasant
> - But what makes the unpleasant into the uncomfortable is some additional factor of mental construction with the perception of an induvidual entitiy
> - Therefore two possibilities:
> 	1. End displeasure
> 		- By elimination
> 			- Eternal heaven
> 		- By precedence (with pleasure or neutrality)
> 			- Eternal heaven
> 	2. End uptake
> 		- Nirvana
