> ## Feeling
> - It seems that a given "locus" of color can be evaluated in three ways. 
> 	- Pleasant, unpleasant, and neutral.
> - It can be modeled as a pixel grid with weights to model attention.
> - In that case, a given locus may be evaluated differently depending on the distribution of weights.
> - Attention may occur in the peripheries and therefore the center need not be at the point of visual focus
> - A red screen may be evaluated differently depending on context and sequence.
> 	- Say one would deliberately make their screen red to evaluate their display's capabilities. In this case they would feel pleasure.
> 	- In another case, their screen would unexpectedly and suddenly turn red, as if something went wrong. In that case they would feel displeasure.
> - Other sensations can affect how a sight is evaluated
> 
> - Does perception and feeling occur simultaneously?
