> ## Mortality and the Futility of Transhumanism
> 
> Even if, with significant technological and scientific advancement, humanity were to reap the fruits of biological immortality, environmental control and automation, virtual realty, and neurological mastery, there still lies the inevitable possibility of death by the physical deterioration and destruction of the body resultant of unforseen tragedies. Currently many thousands of people pass each and every day from unatural death. A single lapse of attention is all that is needed to bring about an inescapable disaster. Even with biological immortality, one would still face escalating existential threats—beginning with Earthly disasters, followed by cosmic events like stellar death, black holes, and ultimately the universe’s demise through heat death, vacuum decay, or the Big Rip. No physical form can indefinitely survive the entropic and structural limits of this physical universe.

> ### Dependant Existence
> 
> It is observed that the typical experience of this world takes place through the five bodily senses and the mind having taken dependence on the brain. While awake, sensations occur primarily through the physical senses and secondarily within the mind in addition to thoughts. At night, sensations occur purely in mind via dreams, and the lack thereof (or at least the lack of the recollection of) in deep sleep.
> 
> With pleasant and unpleasant sensations being categorically stable, happiness purely dependent on favorable experience is totally limited by the nature of the human body and its situation in this world, resulting in an unpredictable and mediocre existence.
> 
> One superior existence is postulated with mind taking dependence on a body with the natural tendency to produce predominantly pleasant sensations situated in a world capable of producing their prerequisites. In other words, a heavenly existence.
> 
> Three opportunities for modification:
> 
> 1.  World - aquire only the desirable
> 2.  Body - process to produce only the desirable
> 3.  Mind - interperet only in a desirable way (perception)
> 
> The relinquishment of desire being a fourth.
