> # Heaven
> 
> I will describe a theoretical heaven in terms of five sensations with thoughts as a sixth. With a being\'s experience centered around their body, equipped with sense faculties.
> 
> Pleasure in every channel of sensation, thoroughly devoid of displeasure. Joined with pleasure, one is continuously satisfied, their thirst in a state of quenching. Devoid of displeasure subject to aversion, they are naturally well free of it too. Thus, with all yearning attenuated below a threshold, how could any negative emotional states arise? Occupied with the five pleasures, how could displeasurable thoughts naturally arise?

> # Slices of the Ultimate
> 
> That the ultimate must be omnipotent, yet it incarnates with a subset of its power as human beings. Using that subset of power, a limited experience is granted. The gods in heaven have a much larger slice of that power, and wield it accordingly.
> 
> The scene is set by the omnipotent, but is experienced in a limited maner. Some control is given to the volition, limited, and the rest is already set, outside of the volitional control.
> 
> So enjoyment, perhaps, is experiencing higher levels closer to the ultimate from a limited perspective. Limitation and separation is pain, like stress built up in the body. Releasing that limitation is enjoyment, like stretching to relieve the stress.
