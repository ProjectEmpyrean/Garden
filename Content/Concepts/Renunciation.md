## `Content/Authors/Shashank Rajesh/Thoughts copy.md`

> # A Heirarchy of Being
> 
> The supreme state of being would be comprised of total detachment, unstruck by any phenomenon. But for those states of being involved in craving, the lower the involvement the better. For craving is a fundamental flaw--its potential symptoms growing more acute the greater it becomes. Therefore, abiding in detachment is the highest ideal, devolving into lower states only if absolutely necessary to maintain the objective.
> 
> ## The Dependence of the Hungry
> 
> Lack is the destablizing factor for those besot by craving. Though they may operate in stability when equippied with their requisites, they are bound to fall when separated from that which they depend on.
