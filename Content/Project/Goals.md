# Goals

Project Empyrean seeks indefinite happiness by using knowledge to guide deliberate choice, in this life and subsequent personal existence. Happiness need only remain above the subjective threshold of complaint; maximizing its intensity is not the goal. Transcending personal existence also qualifies insofar as it eliminates the possibility of unhappiness.

## `Content/Project Empyrean.md`

> - The objective is the attainment of the superior
> - The superior is determined as a result of the functioning of reflective intelligence
> - One reflects that affection underlies all objective pursuits
> 	- When one feels bad by way of appropriated displeasure, they tend to act in avoidance of it, usually in the direction of feeling pleasure.
> 	- When that appropriated displeasure ceases, they feel good, usually by the appropriation of pleasure.
> 	- Thus this twofold pursuit underlies everything that actually matters, as anything only matters on the condition of affection.
> - Therefore they understand that feeling good is the superior. All other notions of superior are underlied by this affective domain.
> - They also understand that volition is the only input to the attainment of the superior
> - So they become heedful to attain the superior
> - In other words, they think, "What will I do to be happy forever?"
> - Thus Project Empyrean seeks the indefinite optimization of volition for the attainment of the superior.
> 
> ## Defining the Superior
> 
> - Stress is inferior, ease is superior.
> - Stress
> 	- Appropriated displeasure
> - Ease
> 	- Appropriated pleasure
> 	- Non-appropriation
> - Both are momentary
> 
> - But consider a sequence of stress and ease across time
> 	- EEESEEE
> 		- Mostly ease with a moment of stress
> 	- EEsEsEE
> 		- A bit more stress but to a lesser degree each time
> - Which is superior?
> - In that case, I would say its arbitrary. Nonetheless, more ease generally superior to more stress.

## `Content/Scratch.md`

> ## A Static Superior
> - What one deems as superior is subject to change. Thus, we would be dealing with a moving target.
> - However, there is only one type of superior which is true and rational: that which concords with the root of affection, desire itself.
> - Thus, the superior is only that which is less stressful.
> - Understanding this, setting the minimization of stress as the static and fundamental principle is rational and sufficient. The goal is no longer subject to momentary opinions. No longer subject to one's addiction to inferior experiences.
