> ### Preservation of Knowlege
> 
> With the intention to make the knowledge of the minimization of suffering available to as many beings as possible, the knowledge uncovered through Project Empyrean is to be preserved in such a way that maximizes its reach to as many beings as possible. One strategy is to encode this information into long lived material compositions with maximal replication and authenticity checking mechanisms through methods such as encryption and zero knowledge proofs. How can we persist this knowledge in its authentic form for as long as possible, and reach as many beings as possible? And perhaps even implement a mechanism for lossless evolution and correction over time?

> ### A endless ripple throughout existence
> 
> With all beings in existence modeled as ignorant entities, making choice after choice causing their own demise, it is possible then to set off a ripple of knowledge that may purify and ultimately liberate all beings in existence? Some sort of a nuclear meltdown of freedom?
