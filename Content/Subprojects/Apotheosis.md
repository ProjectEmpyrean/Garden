> title: Operation Apotheosis
> ---
> - The objective is to transcend human existence indefinitely into a superior plane, so elevated that it might be considered heavenly.
> - And what is a heavenly existence? Simply one considered superior from the human perspective, with pleasure and happiness as the overwhelming default state via the steady acquisition of pleasure, both sensual and non-.
> - And since the superior from the human perspective is indeed anthropomorphic, the heavenly is simply a refined, elevated, sophisticated, and amplified version of ordinary human.
> - Thus we might begin by discerning the typical human paradigm of distress and enjoyment, significantly attenuate or even eliminate the distressful, and then develop and amplify the pleasant.

And so long as these qualities remain, consciousness being bound to this body leaves us with quite an unfortunate situation.
> 
> Normal and supernormal control can provide for a more favorable experience, but is still weighed down by inherent tendecies. Thus, a superior normal and uncontrolled state is sought after -- a heavenly world.
> 
> Craving and sensations seem to operate in a highly complex and interdependent system. This heavanly state of existence would entail a harmonious correlation between these two factors.

> ### Productive Unrestrained Heaven
> 
> Craving is intense, varied, and unstable. High affinity and productivity due to upholding. Variable investment.
> 
> ### Realm of Absorption
> 
> Craving is subtle, uniform, and stable. Very high affinity, perhaps low productiviy due to resting, ok inventment due to residual benefits.
> 
> ### Typical Human
> 
> Intense, varied, and unstable craving with variable affinity, averaging low. Low productivity due to resting, bad investment.
> 
> ### The Ideal Holy Life
> 
> Craving is low, relatively uniform, relatively stable. Mid to high affinity in the beginning. Total affinity at release. Highest productivity and investment in the case this doctrine is true.
