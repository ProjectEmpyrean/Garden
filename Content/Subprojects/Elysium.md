> # A Hypothetical Productive Heaven
> 
> A continuous maintenance of stress below a threshold--utter happiness in a mundane sense. Pleasure delivered continuously to the senses, with boredom offset through automatic or intentionally sought novelty. Whatever desires may arise are swiftly satisfied so that the stress of desire never raises beyond a threshold. And all acts of enjoyment are in some way productive to the maintainence and increase of that sort of existence. Perhaps by relishing in the making of merit. Even better by delighting towards the permanent end of all stress.
> 
> ## Bodily Feelings
> 
> A total absence of bodily pain. There is a continuous flow of baseline bodily pleasure, so much so that it is easy to dwell simply with that pleasure for long periods of time. The environment is such that it is never sensed as too hot nor too cold. The constraint of gravity is overcome so that the body is always light and easily maneuvered.
> 
> ## Sights
> 
> Everything is aesthetically pleasing. There is nothing there that would be seen as ugly or repulsive.
> 
> ## Sounds
> 
> There is a tactile satisfaction to every mundane interaction. No jarring or unpleasant sounds would be found. There is abundant music that is dynamically composed to each moment, constant novelty. No speech that is disagreeable.
> 
> ## Smells
> 
> A baseline pleasantness to all environmental odors. No bad smells.
> 
> ## Tastes
> 
> Good tastes. Everything tastes good or neutral.
> 
> ## Thoughts
> 
> No bad thoughts, the mind dwells in a good place.
