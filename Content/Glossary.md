## Comfort

### `Content/Glossary/Comfort.md`

> Comfort is not feeling bad. Whatever one feels bad about must be felt as unpleasant. Whatever one feels good about must be felt as not unpleasant.
> 
> It should be noted, however, that it is possible to appear to feel good about something unpleasant, but in this case, one doesn't actually *feel* the displeasure significantly. Instead, they feel good about a not unpleasant perception about that displeasure.
> 
> So ultimately, happiness is *not feeling* displeasure beyond a [[Threshold|threshold]].

## Discomfort

### `Content/Glossary/Discomfort.md`

> Unpleasant personally affective experience.
> 
> Two kinds of discomfort:
> 1. One kind that actually sucks
> 2. Another that one actually enjoys, e.g. productive work
> 
> The discomfort here refers only to the first kind. The second would in fact be categorized as [[Comfort]].
